Graduate Diploma of Portfolio Management (BSB80220) – RPL with 3CIR

3CIR offers the Graduate Diploma of Portfolio Management (BSB80220) through Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL). RPL recognises what you already know and can do from your work and life experience, assessed against the requirements of the qualification. It is not a course. It suits people with relevant real-world work experience, especially from military and emergency services backgrounds. RPL is assessed on evidence of your relevant skills and work experience. The free RPL assessment form is the starting point.

How it works: submit the free assessment form, a senior assessor reviews your background, you gather evidence with help from CIRA, your personal RPL mentor, and submit your portfolio through the 3CIR Evidence Portal. Evidence can include position descriptions, training records, performance reviews, work samples and third-party references. The assessor reviews it, confirms the outcome and tells you if anything more is needed.

3CIR is based in Bulimba, Queensland. Qualifications are nationally recognised.
